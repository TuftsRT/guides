# Manage Project

## User Management

As the owner, or authorized approver of a project you can add or remove users. These users have access to all of the resources contained within the project.

## Add Users

### Option 1

Only storage owners and approvers may add users to a project's storage allocation.

1. Go to [Research Computing Resource Manager](https://rcrm.it.tufts.edu/)
1. Click on “Manage” dropdown and select “Projects”
1. Scroll down to "Users" section and click on "Add Users"
1. In the “Search String\*“ input file, copy and paste usernames separated by space or newline for multiple Tufts account searches
1. For “Search by\*”, leave “Exact Username Only“ selected
1. Click on “Search“
1. Under the “Search” button, review Tufts accounts, update “Role” (if necessary), and click on “Add Selected Users to Project“

NOTE: It will take roughly 2 hours for permissions to propagate. If after 2 hours the added users still does not have access, please email tts-research@tufts.edu for support.

### Option 2

Use the Web Form to submit request: [Cluster Storage Request](https://tufts.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5bUmpFT0IXeyEfj)

## Remove Users

### Option 1

Only storage owners and approvers may add users to a project's storage allocation.

1. Go to [Research Computing Resource Manager](https://rcrm.it.tufts.edu/)
1. Click on “Manage” dropdown and select “Projects”
1. Scroll down to "Users" section and click on "Remove Users"
1. Select each user to remove
1. Click on "Remove Selected Users From Project"

### Option 2

Use the Web Form to submit request: [Cluster Storage Request](https://tufts.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5bUmpFT0IXeyEfj)
