#HPC System Choices

This guide is intended to help you choose which HPC systems make sense for your research needs.  Most researchers use a combination of these systems, matching the need of a specific project or task.

TTS Research Technology can help you navigate using all these systems for your research.  When using systems not directly operated by TTS you will need to be increasingly experienced and independent.  Resources available for support and software vary widely across these other systems.

Researchers typically begin using the Tufts HPC cluster for a couple reasons
* Their laptop or workstation is struggling to complete compution in a reasonable time frame
* The pre installed software on the cluster makes it easier than installing, and configuring elsewhere
* they need to perform hundreds or thousands of similar compuations, and want to utilize the scale of a cluster to complete faster
*  Access to technology not readily available elsewhere.  For instance GPUs.
