# Research Storage

TTS Research Computing operates the Tiered Research Storage enviornment to provide storage that meets the unique requirements of researchers.  These include storing large amounts of data, high perfomance access to support HPC, AI and insturment data capture, handling large file counts.  

Sharing data internally and externally

Archiving for long periods of time as required by research or funding agencies

capapable, reiable and cost effective
