# Manage Storage
Currently, there are two options for requesting quota adjustments for Tiered Research Computing Storage.

## OPTION 1: Quota Adjustment Request via RCRM
NOTE: Only storage owners and approvers may submit quota adjustment requests.

1. Go to [Research Computing Resource Manager](https://rcrm.it.tufts.edu/)
1. Click on “Manage” dropdown and select “Projects”
1. Scroll down to "Resource Allocations", locate the row for the desired storage and click "Request Change"
1. Under “Requested Value“, input the new quota in TB
1. Under “Reason for Change“, input reasoning for change
1. Click on “Submit”

If a quota adjustment request is not processed after 24 hours, please email tts-research@tufts.edu for a status update on the quota adjustment request.

## OPTION 2: Quota Adjustment Request via Web Form
