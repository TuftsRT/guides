# Projects

All computing and storage resources are mapped to a organizational unit called a “Project” .  A Project is owned by a single person (Owner).  This owner can designate additional “Authorized Approvers” .  The project has 1 or more “members” whom have access to the data, compute, and other resources allocated to the project.  Each project is assigned to a Dept and a optional Proj/Grant Number for billing purposes.

* Each owner can have one or more Projects.
* All billing rolls up to the Project level.  A projects cost can be split up to 10 ways.
* Faculty can specify which projects thier no cost quota applies to.

Projects are typically owned by a Principle Investigator.  However their are special situations where others are designedated, such as courses where it is the professor of record.  Research Technology may also designate staff or students as owners where they deem apprioriate.
